Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe Disappear — last updated November 19, 2026

The short version

Tic Tac Toe Disappear is guest-only: there are no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. Everything the game remembers stays in your browser's local storage on your device, and the game uses no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.

No accounts

You never create an account or provide an email address, password, or any other personal details to play. Your identity in the game is a random guest identifier plus the display name you choose.

What stays on your device

The game stores the following keys in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless noted below, and clearing your browser storage deletes all of them.

  • tic-tac-toe:guestId — a random identifier generated on first run, used to keep your stats and identity consistent.
  • tic-tac-toe:displayName — the display name you chose.
  • tic-tac-toe:stats:<guestId> — your game statistics (games played, wins, losses, streaks, per-mode breakdown).
  • tic-tac-toe:preferences — your settings, such as theme, sound, and board preferences.
  • tic-tac-toe:language — your UI language choice.
  • tic-tac-toe:onboarding — a flag marking that you completed or skipped the first-run flow.

What is shared

When you play online — in a private room or a quick match — your display name and guest id are sent through the matchmaking relay so the room can show who you are, and your moves and the game state pass through the same relay while the room is open. Your opponent sees your display name; that is the only personal detail shared, and only with the opponent in your room. Your stats, preferences, and language choice are never transmitted, and the relay does not keep room data after the room closes.

No analytics or tracking

The game uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking. No cookies are set for tracking, no fingerprinting takes place, and no data is sold or shared with third parties. The only network traffic the game generates is the online-play connection described above.

Política de privacidad

Las mismas reglas se aplican en español: el juego es solo para invitados, sin cuentas ni registro. Todo lo que el juego recuerda se guarda en el almacenamiento local de tu navegador (identificador de invitado, nombre visible, estadísticas, preferencias e idioma) y no sale de tu dispositivo, salvo el nombre visible y el identificador de invitado, que solo se comparten con tu oponente en una sala en línea. No hay analítica ni seguimiento de terceros. Contacto: privacy@example.com.

Privacy

Tic Tac Toe Disappear — last updated 19 November 2026

Tic Tac Toe Disappear is guest-only: there is no account to create, no sign-up, and no login. Everything the game remembers lives in your browser's local storage on your own device.

What is stored on your device

Under the tic-tac-toe: prefix, local storage holds only what the game needs to work:

  • tic-tac-toe:guestId — a random id that identifies this browser as a guest.
  • tic-tac-toe:displayName — the display name you chose.
  • tic-tac-toe:stats:<guestId> — your game stats (wins, losses, draws).
  • tic-tac-toe:preferences — appearance and opponent settings.
  • tic-tac-toe:language — the interface language.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of it. None of it syncs to a server profile, because there is no account.

What is shared

When you play online, your guest id, display name and moves travel through the shared Cloudflare relay to the opponent in your room — that is how the game reaches them. Your display name is visible to your room opponent only; it is not published, listed, or searchable anywhere. The relay keeps room state only while the room is active and does not build profiles.

What is not collected

There is no third-party analytics or tracking, no advertising, no cookies used for tracking, and no sale or sharing of personal data. The app contacts no service other than the game relay itself.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your data: contact privacy@example.com.

Lo mismo, en español

Tic Tac Toe Disappear es solo para invitados: no hay cuenta que crear ni registro. Todo lo que el juego recuerda se guarda en el almacenamiento local de tu navegador — identificador de invitado, nombre visible, estadísticas, preferencias e idioma — y se borra si limpias los datos del sitio.

Al jugar en línea, tu nombre visible y tus jugadas llegan solo al oponente de tu sala a través del relevo de Cloudflare; el nombre no se publica en ningún sitio. No hay analítica ni rastreo de terceros, ni publicidad, ni cookies de seguimiento.

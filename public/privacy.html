Privacy

Tic Tac Toe Disappear — the three-piece variant. This page states what the app stores, what it shares, and what it never does.

No accounts

The game is guest-only: there are no accounts, no sign-up, no email address, and no password. You play under a randomly generated guest id and a display name you can change at any time.

What stays on your device

Everything the app remembers is written to your browser's local storage on this device, under tic-tac-toe: keys:

  • tic-tac-toe:guestId — your anonymous guest id.
  • tic-tac-toe:displayName — the name shown to opponents.
  • tic-tac-toe:stats:<guestId> — your game stats, keyed by guest id.
  • tic-tac-toe:preferences — theme, sound, colours, shapes, AI difficulty, and opponent name.
  • tic-tac-toe:language — the UI language.

Two small flags live under the same prefix: whether the first-run walkthrough was finished or skipped, and whether social-reaction sounds are muted. Nothing is uploaded to a server profile — clearing your browser's site data erases all of it, and there is no account-side copy to delete.

What is shared

Online play runs over a WebSocket relay operated for this game. When you join a room, your display name and anonymous guest id travel with your moves so the game can label your pieces — your display name is shared with the opponent in your room only, and nowhere else. Local games against the computer or a friend on the same device never leave the device at all.

No analytics, no tracking

There is no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind: no ad networks, no tracking pixels, no behavioural profiles. The app's only network traffic is the game itself — the matchmaking relay that pairs rooms and carries moves between players.
